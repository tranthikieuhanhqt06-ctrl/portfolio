How I think about GEO

01
Answer the real question first

AI systems quote content that states the answer clearly, in the user's own words.

02
Be confirmed by credible sources

Mentions in press and partner sites make a brand safe for AI to cite.

03
Keep brand facts consistent

The same facts about the brand everywhere reduce wrong or missing answers.

04
Measure, don't guess

Track which queries show the brand, and compare against competitors over time.

AI workflows in production

Three of the n8n workflows I built and run. Each one turns a repeated manual task into a pipeline with a human review step before anything is published.

SEO content to WordPressTeachera website

Keyword rows in Google Sheets → fetch reference pages → three Gemini chains write brand-voice body copy with structured output → publish a draft to WordPress → write the link back to the report sheet.

Text to videoPremium Text-to-Video pipeline

Article link or text in → extract content and real images → AI script → voice-over (TTS) → transcription for subtitles → scene spec → render the video → upload to Drive.

Facebook auto-postingTeachera Agency page

On a schedule: read approved posts from Sheets → pull images from Drive → publish single or album posts → mark them as posted. A second flow uses an AI agent with memory to draft posts into the sheet.

How I work

The same four steps across agency, in-house and my own projects.

01

Research & audit

Start from the business goal, then audit the website, channels, competitors and audience. Talk to sales or teachers to hear the questions customers really ask.

02

Plan

Set KPIs, content pillars, channel mix and a monthly calendar in one plan the team can follow. Agree on how results will be reported before work starts.

03

Execute with the team

Write SOPs and brand voice guides, split tasks clearly, and use AI workflows for research and drafts so people spend their time on quality.

04

Measure & improve

Report monthly from Google Analytics, Search Console and Meta, compare against KPIs, keep what works and change what does not.

Clear KPIs from day oneEvery plan starts with numbers both sides agree on
Human reviewAI drafts, people approve everything before it goes live
No fake engagementReal reach, real followers and real leads only
